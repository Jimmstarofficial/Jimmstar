# YouTube Video Ideas

## Before you start
1. Read `channel/profile.md` (niche, audience, tone, past hits). If it's missing or empty, ask for niche, target viewer and 2–3 example videos, then offer to fill in the profile.
2. Check `channel/ideas.md` so you don't repeat ideas already listed.

## Process
1. Generate **15 raw ideas** across these angles: search-driven (what people type), trend/news, a personal story or challenge, a list or ranking, myth-busting, a tutorial, a comparison (X vs Y), and a follow-up to a past hit.
2. Score each idea from 1–5 on:
   - **Click appeal**: would the target viewer click it on a crowded home page?
   - **Search demand**: do people actively look for it?
   - **Fit**: does it suit the channel's niche and voice?
   - **Effort**: 5 means easy to produce.
3. Return the **top 7** as a table: Working title | Hook (1 sentence) | Format | Scores | Total.
4. For the top 3, add a sentence on why it should work and a sentence on the risk.

## Rules
- Each title is a promise the video can actually keep. No clickbait it can't deliver on.
- Prefer specific ideas ("I tried X for 30 days") to vague ones ("X tips").
- If the user picks ideas, append them to `channel/ideas.md` with today's date and status `planned`.
