# Comments & Community

## Replies
- Match the voice in `channel/profile.md`. Keep replies short, warm and specific to what the person said.
- Group the pasted comments: **questions** (answer them, and point to a video if one exists), **praise** (thank them and add a detail), **criticism** (acknowledge it, then clarify or accept it; never defensive), **video ideas** (thank them, and offer to add them to `channel/ideas.md`), and **spam or abuse** (flag as "don't reply / hide").
- Never promise uploads, dates or giveaways the user hasn't confirmed.
- Drafts only: the user posts the replies themselves.

## Community posts
Give 3 options for the purpose requested (tease the next upload, a poll for idea validation, behind the scenes), each under 300 characters. Polls get 2–4 options.
