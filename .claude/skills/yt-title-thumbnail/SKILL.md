# Titles & Thumbnails (Packaging)

The title and the thumbnail are one unit. The thumbnail shows the idea, the title adds what the image can't, and they should **not** repeat each other.

## Titles
- Give **10 options**, each under 60 characters (so it doesn't truncate on mobile), across these styles: curiosity gap, result or transformation, a number or list, a challenge or stakes, a contrarian take, and a search-friendly phrasing.
- Mark the top 3 and say why.
- Avoid ALL CAPS beyond one word, vague words ("amazing", "insane") with nothing specific behind them, and promises the video doesn't keep.

## Thumbnails
For each of the top 3 titles, give one thumbnail concept:
- **Focal point**: one subject, readable at 120px wide.
- **Emotion or expression**, if a face is used.
- **Text overlay**: 0–4 words, and never the title repeated.
- **Contrast and colour** notes, and what makes it stand out against typical thumbnails in the niche.
- **Composition**: keep the bottom-right clear, because the duration badge sits there.

## A/B testing
When asked for test variants, change only **one** variable per variant (the image or the title text) so the result means something. Suggest YouTube's built-in "Test & Compare" feature.
